Hi! I'm a 2nd-year student in the Astrophysics M.S. program at Tufts University, and I received my B.S. from Emory University, majoring in Physics and Astronomy with a minor in Math. I'm currently working on constraining the star formation rate - stellar mass relation for star-forming galaxies using JWST data! In the past, I've also done some research on fitting light profiles of early-type galaxies using GALFIT and emission line modeling for AGN outflows (see my projects section for more details).

Here are some projects I've worked on! For my "Exoplanets and Their Orbits" course, I did a final project taking a look at the stability of disks in the event of a stellar flyby using REBOUND. I was specifically comparing the fraction of escaped planetesimals from a disk around a single star versus a binary star system. I varied both the initial trajectory distance and velocity of the stellar flyby. Generally, I found that the planetesimals around the binary star system were more easily perturbed!
